## Required Changes

1. **Make the caption evidence auditable and stop treating masked tokens as exact words.**  
   T3 lists 13 video IDs, while the farewell analysis uses 12 streams and the introduction describes approximately 58 hours. These could be different subsets, but the draft does not explain them. Add a compact manifest identifying each analyzed stream, date, analyzed interval, and relevant phrase timestamp. State how overlapping caption segments, other speakers, quoted dialogue, and masked words were handled.

   A `[ __ ]` token does not establish *fuck*, much less distinguish “what the fuck” from another expression. “Inferred from frames” is insufficient unless those frames contain a reliable transcript. Identify the actual corroboration or label the reconstruction unverified. Keep authentic profanity uncensored once established.

2. **Remove frequency inflation from the importable card.**  
   In `[SW] Dialogue Style`, “Oh my god every few sentences” is not supported by the stated 6.8 occurrences per hour. In `[SW] Personality`, “runs at full volume” overgeneralizes an excited register. The one-line concept similarly turns a frequent farewell structure into an invariant ending.

   Suggested replacement: “She can accelerate into repeated exclamations and emphatic complaints; ordinary conversation and interview hosting have room for quieter, clearer exchanges.” Retain the reported counts only as explicitly limited sample estimates.

3. **Correct the claim that profanity targets objects rather than people.**  
   Voice Profile and `[SW] Dialogue Style` create an exclusion that the evidence does not justify. The supplied comparison identifies an own-channel Gura-directed insult as a counterexample. That lead requires reconciliation, not omission.

   Replace the exclusion with: “She swears during gaming and can direct playful insults at collaborators; the immediate interaction determines the target and tone.” Do not turn an insult into evidence of lasting hostility.

4. **Separate official phoenix lore from additional supernatural mechanics.**  
   The official profile supports phoenix identity, rebirth, and the exaggerated work-until-rebirth premise. Those should remain. It does not establish the card’s angry-hair combustion, prohibited transformation belt, or an unrestricted mid-concert respawn. [Official Kiara profile](https://hololive.hololivepro.com/en/talents/takanashi-kiara/)

   Locate the public lore statements for the additional mechanics and identify their scope. Until then, keep them in research as unverified. Label the concert-death seed as a proposed fictional situation; it cannot establish instant recovery or consequence-free death.

5. **Keep public relationship comedy explicitly theatrical.**  
   The Calliope row moves from observable wife/family jokes into “still close,” “confronted,” and replacement framing. The card’s family wording can also read as literal biography. Preserve sourced provocative jokes, but describe what happens publicly: mock jealousy, insulting address, or a performed family routine. Remove unsupported conclusions about underlying closeness or motives.

   Likewise, Chonkers and Smoothie must not become fictional “phoenix cats” merely because a reference page discusses publicly mentioned pets. Establish a character-lore source or omit that domestic information from the card.

6. **Repair wording and evidence categories before promoting quotes.**  
   The draft alternates **Krystal** in the stream title and **Crystal** in the purported quotation. Check the game character and recording; annotate an automatic-caption spelling error rather than calling the uncorrected transcription verbatim.

   Separate signature phrases, recurring interaction formats, and individual utterances. “Tell my wife…” and the rhetorical swearing question may be useful contextual examples without being habitual catchphrases. The Doom response needs a locator and should be described as an exaggerated callback, not a psychological condition.

   Apply the four evidence categories consistently, including a research-side map for card claims. Newly introduced likes, dislikes, nervous “hello hello,” and singing/rapping descriptions currently lack sufficiently specific support.

7. **Distinguish vocal observation from interpretation.**  
   The Open Questions admit that pitch and pace were inferred from captions and titles. Those sources cannot verify snorts, breathiness, pitch, or clipped German delivery. Either check representative audio or label the directions as provisional performance interpretations and exclude unsupported acoustic certainty from the card.

   Remove categorical exclusions based on another member’s supposed territory: low deadpan is not exclusively Calliope’s, and quiet speech is not exclusively Ina’s. Keep mismatch checks as warnings against sustained caricature.

8. **Complete the required dossier structure and remove repeated bits.**  
   Restore the boundary/breaking-point entry, the Arc subfields, and the five Secrets/Foreshadowing subfields. “Not established” or “Not applicable” is sufficient where appropriate. Mark all scene seeds as unapproved proposals; the thrown-controller claim also needs evidence or that label.

   Use “(None.)” for Intimacy & Boundaries. Put each fixed joke in its main card field once: exact wording in Catchphrases, speech mechanism in Dialogue Style, and delivery in Voice & Delivery.

- **Everyday connective language:** Repetition, “Okie dokie,” and abrupt tangent starts offer more practical calibration than my comparatively generic examples, once their wording is checked.
