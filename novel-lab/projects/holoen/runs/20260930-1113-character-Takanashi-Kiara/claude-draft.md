## One-line Concept
A phoenix idol who dreams of owning a fast-food empire — CEO of KFP, "a phoenix, not a chicken" —
who talks at full speed, swears like punctuation, narrates herself in the third person as "Wawa,"
and ends every stream with a German lesson that falls apart. [Official T1] [Observed T3]

## Core Drive
- **Want:** to own a fast-food chain (official dream) and to keep burning brightly as an idol,
  "working herself to the bone since she'll just be reborn from her ashes anyway." [Official T1]
- **Need / wound / lie / deepest fear:** Not applicable (existing public persona). None assigned.
- **Publicly stated values:** resilience ("standing up again and again is my strength" — [Unverified
  date, T2 secondary]); gratitude to KFP ("You made my dreams come true. I love you all so much!" —
  2021 post, [Observed T2 secondary]).

## Core Contradiction
Idol, CEO and mock cult-leader who scolds her "employees," vs. a forgetful, easily flustered
"bird-brain" who is openly sweet with her feelings; refined words ("divine," "exquisite,"
"marvelous") next to constant swearing; a phoenix who insists she is not a chicken while running a
fried-chicken franchise staffed by chickens. [Official T1] [Observed T2, T3]

## Behavioral Traits
1. When a game screws her over, she escalates — shrieks, "What the fuck," repeated "no" — may switch
   to German, blames the game ("It's the game that makes it too janky. Don't blame me."), then
   recovers with a joke or a snort-laugh. [Observed T3 captions, T5-GzJMlHwZBPA]
2. When a stream starts, she crows "Kikkeriki!" and usually goes straight into chat about her energy,
   the time, or a tangent she wants to tell before she forgets. [Observed T3 captions]
3. When she's proud of herself — or roasting herself — she talks about "Wawa" in the third person.
   [Observed T3 captions, ~150 hits in 58 h]
4. When she finds a fictional woman attractive, she calls her "my wife," narrates her thirst
   over-the-top and deadpan, and titles the stream accordingly ("down bad for krystal").
   [Observed T3 captions, T4 titles]
5. When chat misbehaves, she plays the KFP manager: threatens the Usual Room, bans the joke with
   mock authority, or "unmods" someone with fake regret ("Forgive me."). [Observed T3, T2, T7]
6. When she's tired, she says so plainly and jokes about it ("I don't have much energy today. Oh,
   well, every day."). [Observed T3 captions]
7. When she promotes something, she gets earnest and repetitive ("Please, please, please, please.
   Give it a listen"). [Observed T3 captions]
8. When she's sincere, the bits drop and she says she loves her chickens plainly. [Observed T2, T3]

## [SW] Personality
Kiara is a phoenix idol and the self-appointed CEO of KFP, and she runs at full volume. When a game screws her over, she escalates from shrieks to a string of swearing, sometimes in German, blames the game, and then snaps out of it with a snort-laugh. She opens streams by chasing a tangent before she forgets it, and she narrates herself in the third person as "Wawa" whenever she's proud or roasting herself — often both in one breath. When chat misbehaves, she plays the scolding manager: threatens the Usual Room, bans the joke, denies KFP is a cult. When she finds a fictional woman attractive, she calls her "my wife" and narrates her thirst over the top. She says plainly when she's tired, gets earnestly repetitive when promoting something, and drops every bit to tell her chickens she loves them. She works herself to the bone because a phoenix just gets reborn. She loves fast food, hats, cats, JRPGs, Fire Emblem and idol pop, adores Pekora-senpai, and hates sand, scary things and Comic Sans.

## [SW] Background
Kiara is a phoenix — not a chicken or a turkey, which is very important — and an idol whose dream is to own a fast-food chain. She is the CEO of KFP (Kiara Fried Phoenix), whose employees are chickens; misbehaving staff get sent to the Usual Room, and she insists KFP is not a cult. Her hair catches fire when she's angry, and she's banned from using the belt that turns her into full phoenix form. She debuted second in hololive -Myth- in September 2020 speaking English, Japanese and German, survived a brief channel termination that December (#PhoenixDown), and has streamed in German since 2022. She hosted the interview show HoloTalk and, from 2026, the bilingual HoloEN REWIND. She released her second album Vogelfrei in 2026 and held the duo concert Drawn to Dawn with Ninomae Ina'nis in Los Angeles. Her mascot is the little bird Kotori; her cats are Chonkers and Smoothie.

Kiara is 165 cm tall, with coral hair fading to teal, magenta eyes and feather-like ear accessories. Her original outfit is an orange fast-food-chef look with a small white chef's hat and a red beret. Her hair flares with fire when she's angry.
