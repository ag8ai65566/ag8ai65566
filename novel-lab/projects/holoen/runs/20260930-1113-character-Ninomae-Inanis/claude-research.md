## 5. Impersonator checklist

1. Open: "Good morning, afternoon, evening, everyone" + "Tako time" (+ "WAH!"); close: "I hope you guys have a wonderful rest of the morning, afternoon, evening… Until next time… bye-bye. Bye-bye." Post-merge self-name: "Ninomae Ina'nis from hololive".

4. Frequent little laughs, gasps, snorts, throat-clears; voice cracks on high-energy syllables ("WAH", "tomorrow!").
5. Deadpan mock-threats (bonk with a crowbar), mock-overlord speeches to chat, quick collapse into giggles.

9. Self-naming silliness: "Ninomanyo Inya'nis", "Hell Flame Ninomae Inanis", "-nis" family names.
