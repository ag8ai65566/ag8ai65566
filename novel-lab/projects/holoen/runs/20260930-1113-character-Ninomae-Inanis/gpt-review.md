## Required Changes

1. **Keep explicitly unverified wording out of the verified phrase inventory.**  
   Voice Profile labels “Warau na!” and “Oh, crab” as wiki-uncited, yet `[SW] Catchphrases` presents both as established. The full Forbidden WAH sentence follows the same path. Open Question 1 does not override the existing evidence rule.

   Locate supporting public recordings or reliable contextual transcriptions. Otherwise retain those exact formulations only in research as unverified. Preserve the bawdy WAH concept where supported; uncertainty about a complete sentence is not a reason to sanitize the underlying joke.

2. **Correct the greeting attribution and composite quotation labels.**  
   The official profile’s greeting is different from the combined stream-opening sequence in Voice Profile and Sample Line 1. Keep the profile formulation and the reported spoken variant separately attributed. The official page also confirms **一伊那尓栖**, resolving Open Question 2. [Official Ina profile](https://hololive.hololivepro.com/en/talents/ninomae-inanis/)

   A sequence assembled from I1 and I3 is a composite, not one verbatim utterance. Separate the excerpts with their own locators or label the assembled line as a style example. Automatic captions should be identified as caption transcriptions until checked.

3. **Remove unsupported acoustic certainty.**  
   A birthday title written with stutters does not establish the recording’s breathiness, pitch, or actual stammer. A written priestess introduction does not establish a low, ominous delivery. Bijou pitching her voice down for an impression is not a measurement of Ina’s vocal register.

   In Voice Profile, Tone Shifts, and `[SW] Voice & Delivery`, distinguish observed audio from suggested interpretation. Change “excitement lifts her pitch until it cracks” to an occasional possibility supported by the particular reaction. Remove the generalized horror calm unless representative recordings support it.

4. **Scope frequency claims and preserve the continuous persona.**  
   The “like” ratio needs the analyzed intervals, token count, treatment of repeated caption windows, and speaker attribution. Two chat streams do not by themselves characterize every context.

   “Humu humu … now rare” needs comparative evidence and is unnecessary in the card. Retain the expression with its listening function. Historical dates for the chant or Mario Kart callback can remain in research; they should not imply that the character has lost access to those memories.

5. **Avoid turning low profanity into an innocence rule.**  
   “Essentially none,” “wink-level,” and the automatic surprise at “damn” collectively narrow her permissible register beyond the presented evidence. Her reported self-description can be retained as a contextual statement, not a universal guarantee.

   Use wording such as: “Her ordinary speech often favors mild exclamations; sharper language and bawdy wordplay can occur in specific exchanges.” Check the keyboard-incident lead from my draft before adding **ass** or its full sentence. Do not add stronger vocabulary merely for balance, and do not euphemize verified wording.

Separate the priestess premise from publicly stated creative and audience-facing priorities. Her interview supports concrete ambitions in creative work and charity.
