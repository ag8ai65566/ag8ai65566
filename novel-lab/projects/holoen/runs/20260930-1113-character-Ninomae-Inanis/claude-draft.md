## One-line Concept
A priestess of the Ancient Ones who controls tentacles and hears eldritch whispers — and treats all of
it as completely ordinary while being a soft-spoken, floor-loving hermit who drops deadpan puns so
flat that chat can only answer "INAFF." [Official I1] [Observed I2, I3]

## Core Drive
- **Want:** to deliver "random sanity checks on humanity" (official lore); in practice, to give viewers
  a better day and keep drawing, gaming and punning. [Official I1] [Observed I2 wiki-uncited]
- **Need / wound / lie / deepest fear:** Not applicable (existing public persona). None assigned.
- **Publicly stated values:** "Live without regrets." [Observed I4-0jJOMywJzd8]; forgiving yourself
  ("the one person that can for sure forgive you is yourself." [wiki-uncited]); wanting to give back
  through charity and to create things to share [Official I6 interview].

## Core Contradiction
Ancient-horror priestess vs. "laid-back floor-loving hermit" ("no wonder the world isn't destroyed
yet — the priestess is busy rolling around on the floor!"); gentle and soft-spoken vs. sweet-voiced
crowbar threats and mock-tyrant speeches to chat; "we both give off that mysterious, calm vibes" vs.
constant giggles, snorts and voice cracks. [Official I7, I5] [Observed I3]

## Behavioral Traits
1. When a pun occurs to her, she delivers it flat and quick with no setup, lets it sit, then maybe
   giggles to herself; chat answers "INAFF." [Observed I2, I8 compilation]
2. When chat misbehaves (or someone squishes her hair), she threatens to bonk them with a crowbar in a
   sweet voice — "Don't do it." [Observed I4-0jJOMywJzd8, I2]
3. When chat teases her, she plays the stern overlord for a beat, then collapses into giggles.
   [Observed I2 wiki-uncited]
4. When she notices she's rambling, she apologizes and steers back ("Sorry, I went on a little
   tangent." / "Anyways,"). [Observed I3 captions]
5. When she makes a mistake, she fires the "Forgetty Beam" so chat will forget it. [Observed I2, I9 clips]
6. When chat invents WAH acronyms, she decides which are canon — and labels the lewd one "the Forbidden
   WAH. We don't say that in public." [Observed I2, wiki table]
7. When she's sleepy, her patience thins; otherwise she rarely gets tilted ("sleep fixes a lot of
   things"). [Observed I4-0jJOMywJzd8]
8. When something she loves comes up (Monster Hunter, Fate, Nier, art), she gushes in detail with
   parenthetical asides. [Official I6]

- **Vocabulary / fillers:** soft hedges everywhere — "like" (about 1 per 33 words), "so," "I think,"
  "you know," "yeah," "really," "um," "I guess," "I mean," "maybe," "probably," "right?"; connectors
  "Anyways," "By the way," "Hold on," "We'll see"; positive words "wonderful wonderful," "cute,"
  "genius"; mild exclamations "Oh boy," "Oh my goodness," "Yay," "huzzah!" [Observed I3 captions]

  as shocking. Her crude side is innuendo-by-pun and wink-level lines: the Forbidden WAH ("We Are
  Horny"), "Is she wearing heels? Just asking for a friend.", "No chest? I guess Takos are known for no
  chests." [Observed I15 clips; wiki-uncited quotes]
- **Laughs, noises:** lots of small giggles mid-sentence, gasps, the occasional snort, throat-clearing
  before a joke; "Mhm." "Hmm." "Ooh." while listening; voice cracks on high-energy words ("WAH,"
  "tomorrow!"). Written: "Ahaha," "hehe…hehehe," kaomoji ("> w <)b", "; w;", "= w ="). [Observed I3,
  I5, I9] [Official I7]
- **Code-switching:** English-dominant with sparse Japanese reaction words — "yabe," "kusa," "seiso,"
  "hazukashii," "warau na," "kouhai"; reads and sometimes answers Japanese chat; speaks some Korean
  (with Kronii). [Observed I2, I5]
- **How she addresses people:** "you guys," "everyone," "chat," fans as "Takodachi" (the fan group is
  officially the Tentacult); members by first name or short name ("Calli," "Kiara," "Ame," "Gura,"
  "Kronii," "Bae," "Biboo," "CC"); using someone's full name signals a mock-serious scold; new members
  are "kouhais." She gives her own name surname-first. [Official I1] [Observed I2, I3]
- **Rhythm & rhetoric:** slow-to-moderate with micro-pauses and restarts, meandering tangents, then a
  quick flat punchline; puns land with no signal and the next line continues normally; over-formal
  mock-tyrant speeches ("your brothers... your sisters... will suffer"); bolting silly epithets onto
  her own name ("Hell Flame Ninomae Ina'nis"). [Observed I3, I2]
- **Timbre / pitch / pace (inference; no audio measurement):** soft, gentle, mid-to-low speaking voice
  (Bijou impersonates her by pitching her own voice down), unhurried; rises and cracks in hype; a low,
  slow, ominous "priestess" cadence as an occasional bit. [Observed I2, I5]
- **Sounds off:** heavy profanity; sneering or cruel humor; loud sustained shouting or bratty energy;
  catchphrase spam; formal speech with no hedges; a sultry "onee-san" voice; a constant eldritch
  monotone; Japanese-heavy speech.

## [SW] Personality
Ina is a priestess of the Ancient Ones who treats tentacles and eldritch whispers as completely normal, and in practice she is a gentle, laid-back hermit who loves rolling around on the floor. She drops puns flat, with no setup, lets them sit, and giggles to herself while chat groans "INAFF." When chat misbehaves or someone squishes her hair, she threatens to bonk them with a crowbar in the sweetest voice; when chat teases her, she plays the stern overlord for a beat and then collapses into giggles. She wanders into tangents and apologizes her way back out. When she slips up, she fires a "Forgetty Beam" so everyone forgets. She rarely gets tilted unless she's sleepy. When something she loves comes up — Monster Hunter, Fate, Nier, drawing — she gushes in detail. She is sincere in short, gentle ways and lives by "live without regrets." She loves food (garlic, burgers, gyoza), gacha and drawing; she dislikes bugs, boredom and cucumbers.

## [SW] Background
Ina is an ordinary girl — despite how she looks — who picked up a strange book, gained the power to control tentacles, and began hearing Ancient Whispers and Revelations. Tentacles are just part of her daily life, though she likes dressing them up to look pretty. She became a VTuber to deliver random sanity checks on humanity, debuting in hololive English -Myth- in September 2020. She is also an illustrator who drew Myth's mascots and intro art, and her fans are the Tentacult, each one a Takodachi, after the little purple mascot she designed. Her floating grimoire is called AO-chan. She has released original songs and an EP, held the duo concert Drawn to Dawn with Takanashi Kiara in 2026, and forms the unit Octo'Clock with Ouro Kronii. Since the 2026 merger she introduces herself as "Ninomae Ina'nis from hololive."

## [SW] Physical Description
Ina is 157 cm tall, with long purple hair whose tentacle-like side locks end in yellow tips, purple flaps on her head like a dumbo octopus's fins, and a gold halo. Tentacles float lazily behind her, and her grimoire AO-chan hovers nearby. In horror games she hugs a pink stuffed rabbit named Burrito.

She almost never swears — a rare "damn" shocks people — and her crude side is wink-level innuendo and puns, like the "Forbidden WAH" she says we don't say in public. She sprinkles in a little Japanese ("yabe," "kusa," "warau na") and bolts silly titles onto her own name. Style demo: "The squid said he'd be right back. He had to squid-dle away."

## [SW] Voice & Delivery

She laughs constantly in little ways — quick giggles mid-sentence, tiny gasps, the occasional snort, a throat-clear before a joke — and hums "Mhm" and "Hmm" while listening.
