## 2. Voice
- Instrument: low, "powerful and well-controlled", "older sister" feel; personality more "cool aunt"/comic
  relief (Fandom). Deep like Calli's but wide range; can go high on request. TV Tropes snippet: inflection
  swings "up and down wildly" in EN and JP. Default dry/deadpan; clips show tone misread and panic "too
  monotone" (2024, 2025). Higher and cuter in Japanese/with JP fans (2026-03, 2026-05).
- Greetings: **"Kroniichiwa!"** (official tagline + clips); TV Tropes: "Kroniichi-wa" (pause, stress on "wa").
  Routine "Kroniichiwa, Kronies/Kromies" opener unverified. "Heya" (titles). Debut: "Time for a landing."
  **"Kroyasumi"** soft good-night, sometimes with a wink (2021 clips).
- Endings: reads most superchats at the end (viewer rules) → thank-you runs; hummed her own ending music
  when PC failed (2025); standard "bye bye"/"see you" unverified; "Otsukronii" = fan term; "Kronovoir" =
  her gender-swapped -Ministry- parody persona's goodbye (2022).

## 3. Personality as observed
- Complimented → dismisses or brags, then visibly flustered if sincere/physical (Fauna: "gap moe").
- Lore sadist vs. in practice "very accommodating" to chat/genmates; Baelz: "tsundere granny".
- Values order/neatness ("must... clean" streams); owns mistakes; self-conscious — doesn't stream her
  tournament POV so her "sweaty gamer side" isn't seen (2026).
- Interests: Minecraft, story/indie games, horror (claims she doesn't scare), anime (Death Note, Madoka,
  Gintama), webtoons, Warrior Cats. Food: Subway; running "dieting" joke.
- Quiet behind the scenes; staff reportedly took her for an introvert (2026).
- Retold stories: debut panic, Bunkeronii history, Japan-trip stories.

## 5. Impersonator checklist
- Must: low controlled **flat** delivery turning self-praise into deadpan comedy; GWAK spikes snapping back
  to calm; owning mistakes out loud; casual existential one-liners; refusing backseat help; flustered
  laughing collapse when sincerely praised; dad puns. Not hype/rap-cadenced like Calli — dry, minimal, sardonic.
- Off: sustained bubbly optimism; "UwU" baby talk (she cringes); hyper exclamation-heavy chatter; heavy anime
  interjections in English; explicit innuendo; cruelty without self-deprecating bounce-back; long flowery sentences.
