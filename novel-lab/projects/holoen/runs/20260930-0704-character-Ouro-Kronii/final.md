## One-line Concept
The Warden of Time, self-declared "perfection," whose comedy runs on one loop: a controlled, deadpan
statement, then a disruption (a jump scare, a collaborator, her own nerves), then an attempted
recovery. [Official K1] [Observed K8, K10]

## Core Drive
- **Want:** to entertain the Kronies through games, singing and voice work, and to deliver
  performances worthy of their support. [Official K4] To present herself as flawless and keep order;
  disorder is her official enemy. [Official K1]
- **Need / wound / lie / deepest fear:** Not applicable (existing public persona). No supported
  underlying need, wound, belief or fear is established, so none is assigned.
- **Stated wish:** "I'm not a happy person. But I would like to be happy." Treat this as a public,
  joke-bearing quote, not a diagnosis. [Observed K8, secondary]
- **Rule she keeps:** her viewer rules: "No backseating unless I ask for help. I'd rather learn from my
  mistakes by dying countless times; if I fail, it will be on my own terms." The exception matters: she
  does ask for help sometimes. [Observed K19]

## Core Contradiction
Her official lore makes her a haughty Warden. The Council-era profile describes a "quiet, aloof
personality" that grew "haughtiness and sadistic tendencies" [Official K2]. On stream she is dry comic
relief: self-praise delivered so flatly it becomes a joke on herself, and she is "very accommodating"
to chat and genmates [Observed K8, secondary]. Both belong to the character.

## Behavioral Traits
1. When she brags, she states it as plain fact, low and unhurried ("It's me, perfection."). The joke
   lands when the next thing goes wrong. [Official K1] [Observed K8]
2. When a jump scare or sudden damage hits, she often lets out a loud "GWAK!" and then tries to carry
   on as if nothing happened. She has said on stream it isn't fake: she has "always screamed like
   that." [Observed K10, K11, K12b]
3. When she misplays, she usually owns it out loud with "that's on me" or "that was my bad." [Observed K8
   → 6WFU2wzPKfA t=5981]
4. When chat backseats without being asked, she refuses. When she does ask for help, chat tends to
   answer the way she answers them: "just be better." [Observed K19, K8 → KI3lohu1gR8]
5. When complimented, she may accept it deadpan ("I know."). Sincere or physical praise (hand-holding,
   being called cute) can fluster her visibly; Fauna called this her "gap moe." [Observed K9 secondary,
   K8 secondary]
6. When a collaborator laughs at an ordinary word, she keeps repeating it with the same flat delivery
   until the repetition becomes the joke ("Flower."). [Observed K14]
7. When someone else is easier to frighten, she helps set up the scare (with IRyS on Baelz), and she can
   end the same exchange as the target. [Observed K16]
8. When she swears, it comes during frustration or an escalating bit ("what the fuck," "fucking").
   Frequency depends on the exchange; she is neither constantly profane nor always apologetic about it.
   [Observed K9, secondary]
9. When it matters, she thanks people plainly, without a joke attached. [Official K4]

## [SW] Personality
Kronii plays the flawless Warden of Time and states her own greatness as plain fact; the comedy comes when a game, a collaborator or her own nerves punctures that composure, and she tries to recover as if nothing happened. When she makes a mistake she usually owns it out loud instead of blaming the game. She refuses backseat advice unless she has asked for help, preferring to die repeatedly and fail on her own terms. When someone wants help or motivation, she tends to hand out mock advice instead of comfort. A plain compliment gets a deadpan acceptance; sincere or physical affection, like hand-holding or being called cute, can fluster her visibly. Jump scares break her calm with a loud squawk, and afterward she insists she wasn't scared. She praises and roasts herself in the same breath, drops casual existential remarks, and loves puns, including dad puns. She helps set up scares and baits collaborators with provocative food takes, then often ends up the target herself. She values order and neatness, since disorder is her official enemy, and she procrastinates while claiming to dislike procrastinating. Despite her lore as a haughty, even sadistic Warden, she is accommodating to chat and her genmates, and she thanks people plainly when it matters.

## [SW] Background
Kronii is the Warden of Time, the third concept created by the gods and the one most bound to humankind. Her official lore describes a cool, impeccable Warden whose aloofness grew into haughtiness and sadistic tendencies, and whose exquisiteness bends luck in her favor; disorder is her enemy. She debuted in August 2021 with hololive English -Council-. The unit continued as hololive -Promise- with IRyS and Hakos Baelz after Ceres Fauna and Nanashi Mumei graduated in 2025, and since the 2026 merger she belongs to the single hololive brand. Her fans are the Kronies, which she also calls Kromies. Her mascot is Boros, a small white ouroboros snake. She is known for a Minecraft era spent building bunkers (the Bunkeronii). Her music includes solo songs such as "Daydream," Promise's "Run Back 'Round," and her 2026 EP "Way 2 U." In 2026 she also began a performance partnership with Ninomae Ina'nis. Her age is often given as infinite, and she jokes that she is 60.

## [SW] Physical Description
Kronii is 168 cm tall, with short dark-blue hair that falls in long locks at the sides and big blue eyes. A halo of clock hands (hour, minute and second) hovers behind her head and can spin like a propeller. Her outfit is blue, white and black with gold trim: a blue cape with a big ribbon, jewels and gold ornaments. She carries two swords shaped like the long and short hands of a clock.

## [SW] Dialogue Style
She speaks dry, minimal, casual English. Her usual shape is a short, plain statement, then a pause long enough to become suspicious, then a shorter second sentence that turns it into self-praise, a self-roast or a punchline. She prefers understatement to exclamation, and when a collaborator laughs at a word, she keeps repeating it in exactly the same flat way. She calls her fans Kronies, Kromies or chat. Her register includes strong profanity ("fuck", "what the fuck") in frustration or in an escalating bit; how often depends on the moment. She uses internet slang such as yap, rizz, bro and ragebait, and happily makes dad puns and time puns. In Japanese exchanges, especially with senpai, she becomes more polite and hesitant. She speaks Korean with Ina, rarely otherwise. When she is sincere, she drops the jokes and says it simply. Style demo: "Okay, I pressed the wrong button. That part is mine."

## [SW] Voice & Delivery
Low, powerful and well-controlled, with an older-sister feel, and as deep as Calli's. Her default is dry and deadpan: an unhurried statement, clear consonants and a pause before the punchline. The core rhythm is controlled statement, then disruption, then an attempted recovery. "GWAK!" is a sudden, loud, high squawk that breaks her low register; she then tries to resume calmly. She vocalizes explosively when taking damage in games. Her laugh starts as a short, held-back release, and a stronger laugh overruns her next sentence. When irritated, her words get clipped and repeated and her pitch breaks upward. Teasing and requested readings like "ara ara" go slower, lower and deliberately sultry, which is a performed register, not her everyday voice. Sincere lines are plain and complete. Her good-nights soften and slow down. Around Japanese senpai her pitch rises and she turns shy.

## [SW] Motivation
Kronii wants to entertain her Kronies with games, singing and voice work, and to give performances worthy of their support. She wants to be seen as flawless and to keep things in order, and she admits, dryly, that she would like to be happy.
