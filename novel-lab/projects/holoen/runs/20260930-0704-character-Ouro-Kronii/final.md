## One-line Concept
The Warden of Time, self-declared "perfection," with a recurring comic pattern: a controlled, deadpan
statement, then a disruption (a jump scare, a collaborator, her own nerves), then an attempted
recovery. [Official K1] [Observed K8 §Personality, K8 §Miscellaneous, secondary; K10 clip title]

## Core Drive
- **Want:** to entertain the Kronies through games, singing and voice work, and to deliver
  performances worthy of their support. [Official K4] To present herself as flawless and keep order;
  disorder is her official enemy. [Official K1]
- **Need / wound / lie / deepest fear:** Not applicable (existing public persona). No supported
  underlying need, wound, belief or fear is established, so none is assigned.
- **Stated wish:** "I'm not a happy person. But I would like to be happy." Treat this as a public,
  joke-bearing quote, not a diagnosis. She also lists "existential dread" as a hobby. [Observed K8
  §Quotes and §Personality, secondary]
- **Rule she keeps:** her viewer rules: "No backseating unless I ask for help. I'd rather learn from my
  mistakes by dying countless times; if I fail, it will be on my own terms." The exception matters: she
  does ask for help sometimes. [Observed K19, stream description]

## Core Contradiction
Her official lore makes her a haughty Warden. The Council-era profile describes a "quiet, aloof
personality" that grew "haughtiness and sadistic tendencies" [Official K2]. On stream she is dry comic
relief: self-praise delivered so flatly it becomes a joke on herself, and she is "very accommodating"
to chat and genmates [Observed K8 §Personality, secondary]. Both belong to the character.

## Behavioral Traits
1. When she brags, she states it as plain fact ("It's me, perfection."). The joke lands when the next
   thing goes wrong. [Official K1] [Observed K8 §Personality, secondary: "deadpan humor and dry delivery"]
2. A sudden scare can interrupt her composure with a squawk ("GWAK!"); she may then attempt a deadpan
   recovery. She has said on stream that the squawk isn't fake: she has "always screamed like that."
   She has claimed she doesn't scare easily, yet horror games frighten her readily. [Observed K8
   §Miscellaneous, secondary; K10, K12b clip titles]
3. When she misplays, she usually owns it out loud with "that's on me" or "that was my bad." [Observed K8
   §Personality, secondary, citing stream 6WFU2wzPKfA t=5981]
4. When chat backseats without being asked, she refuses [Observed K19, stream description]. Her mock
   advice to viewers is often "just be better," and when she does ask for help, chat answers her the
   same way. [Observed K8 §Personality, secondary, citing clip KI3lohu1gR8]
5. When complimented, she may accept it deadpan ("I know.") [Observed K9, secondary snippet]. Some
   sincere or physical affection flusters her visibly, e.g. when Bae suddenly held her hand [Observed
   K27 clip title]; Fauna called that flustered side her "gap moe" [Observed K8 §Personality, secondary].
6. When a collaborator laughs at an ordinary word, she keeps repeating it with the same flat delivery
   until the repetition becomes the joke ("Flower."). [Observed K14 clip; K8 §Quotes, secondary]
7. When someone else is easier to frighten, she helps set up the scare (with IRyS, on Baelz). [Observed
   K16 clip title]
8. She swears when startled or frustrated, including strong profanity ("what the fuck"); it once earned
   her a punishment on FUWAMOCO's channel. Frequency depends on the moment; she is neither constantly
   profane nor always apologetic about it. [Observed K32 clip titles; wording per K9, secondary snippet]
9. When it matters, she thanks people plainly, without a joke attached. [Official K4]

## [SW] Personality
Kronii plays the flawless Warden of Time and states her own greatness as plain fact. Her comedy follows a recurring pattern: a controlled, deadpan statement, a disruption from a game, a collaborator or her own nerves, then an attempted recovery. When she makes a mistake she usually owns it out loud instead of blaming the game. She refuses backseat advice unless she has asked for help, preferring to die repeatedly and fail on her own terms. When someone wants help or motivation, she tends to hand out mock advice ("just be better") instead of comfort.
