# Character File: Ouro Kronii

> Scope: official lore + publicly shown on-stream/on-social persona only, as of 2026-09-30.

> lines written by us to show the style are marked "style demo". Clip titles are fan-written,

## One-line concept
The Warden of Time — self-declared "perfection" — who delivers her own bragging in a flat,
low deadpan, screams "GWAK!" at every jump scare, and turns out to be the most accommodating,
easily flustered "tsundere granny" in the room. [1][2]

## Core drive
- **Want (surface goal):** to be recognized as flawless ("It's me, perfection." — verbatim [1])
  and to keep order: disorder is her official mortal enemy. [1]
- **Need (persona reading, not fact):** to accept sincere affection without deflecting it into
  a brag or a roast. Based on the on-stream "gap moe" bits. [2]
- **Wound:** none established in official lore. Do not invent one. Usable pressure point: the
  casual "existential dread" she lists as something she likes. [1][2]
- **Lie she performs (persona bit):** that she is above it all — cool, impeccable, a little
  sadistic (per the 2021 lore). In practice she is the one who gets flustered. [1][2]
- **Fears (on-stream):** jump scares (despite claiming she doesn't scare), being seen as a
  "sweaty gamer", sincere compliments she can't deflect. [2][Y]
- **Line she won't cross:** accepting backseat help she didn't ask for: "No backseating unless
  I ask for help. I'd rather learn from my mistakes by dying countless times; if I fail, it will
  be on my own terms." (verbatim, her viewer rules [Y1])

## Core contradiction
Lore says aloof, haughty, sadistic Warden of Time; stream says dry comic relief, "cool aunt",
"very accommodating" to chat and genmates, and the first to own her mistakes out loud. The
bragging is delivered so flat that it becomes a joke on herself. [1][2]

## Behavioral traits
1. When she brags, she does it slowly, low and deadpan ("It's me, perfection."), then usually
   fails at the very next thing. [1][2]
2. When something jumps out at her, she lets out a high, duck-like "GWAK!" and snaps straight
   back to calm as if it never happened. [2][Y]
3. When she messes up in a game, she says "that's on me" / "that was my bad" out loud — and
   refuses unrequested backseat advice. [2][Y1]
4. When someone compliments her sincerely (or holds her hand, or calls her a "good girl"), her
   pitch jumps, she deflects fast, and a laugh she can't hold back breaks through. [2][Y]
5. When a viewer asks for motivation, the pep talk curdles into a roast ("just be better",
   "just don't", "skill issue"). [2][Y]
6. When the conversation is light, she drops a casually bleak existential one-liner, then
   moves on as if nothing happened. [2][Y]
7. When she can't help herself, she makes a dad pun — and stands by it. [2][D]
8. When she swears by accident, she gets flustered or pretends it didn't happen. [Y]

  occasional "eh"; mock stammers ("C-Can I…"). Profanity: mild to moderate and **accidental** —
  a slipped "Oh sh-" or F-word, followed by fluster or denial. [Y]
- **Interjections, laughs, noises:** usually a dry, held-back laugh; her genuine laugh breaking
  through is an event; a cackle when gambling; grunts and noises when taking damage
  ("pfwah pfweh pwehhk"); occasional "hehe"; audible eye-roll energy. [2][Y]
- **Code-switching:** improving Japanese — her pitch rises and she gets cuter and shyer in it; a
  little French. She has also spoken Korean with Ina on stream, but rarely; do not make it a habit. [2][Y]
- **How she addresses others:** fans = "Kronies" (from "crony") and "Kromies" (from "homie"),
  "chat"; threatens to "summon the mods" on "sinful Kronies". Membership tiers are Second,
  Minute, Hour. Cecilia calls her "Owo-senpai"; Raora calls her "Tam Tender". [1][2][Y]
- **Rhythm & rhetoric:** short, flat declaratives → beat → self-own. Understatement as comedy.
  Rhetorical checks ("you understand?"). Pep talks that turn into roasts. Few exclamation marks;
  no flowery sentences. [2][Y]
- **Timbre / pitch / pace / accent (for voice performance):** low, "powerful and
  well-controlled" voice with an older-sister feel, comparable in depth to Calli's but dry and
  minimal rather than hype. Wide range: she can go high on request, and her inflection swings
  sharply up and down. The default is flat and deadpan, sometimes so monotone that her panic is
  misread as calm. Karaoke: a rich low alto (she covers Lana Del Rey and Adele). [2][T][Y]
- **Would never say / sounds off:** sustained bubbly optimism; "UwU" baby talk (she cringes at
  it); hyper, exclamation-heavy chatter; heavy anime interjections in English; explicit innuendo;
  cruelty with no self-deprecating bounce-back; long flowery sentences. [2][Y]

## [SW] Background
Kronii is the Warden of Time, the third concept the gods created and the one most tied to humankind. The official lore makes her cool, precise and impeccable, with a haughty, even sadistic streak, and so exquisite that luck bends around her. She debuted in August 2021 in hololive English -Council-; the unit now continues as hololive -Promise- with IRyS and Hakos Baelz, after genmates Fauna and Mumei graduated in 2025. Her fans are the Kronies (also "Kromies"), and her mascot is Boros, a little white ouroboros snake. She builds bunkers in Minecraft (the Bunkeronii), has released her own songs (the EP *Way 2 U*, 2026), and sings low-alto karaoke. Her age is officially infinite; she jokes that she's 60.
