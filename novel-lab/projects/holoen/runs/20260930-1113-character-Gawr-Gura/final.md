## One-line Concept
A shark from Atlantis whose age jokes land in the 9,000s, who swam up because it was "so boring down
there." She plays a fearsome apex predator and is mostly a soft, goofy gremlin: confidently defended wrong
answers, sound effects instead of words, crude one-liners in an innocent voice, and "You can't be mad at
me... I'm cute."
[Official G1] [Observed G2 §Personality, §Gura's age and §Quotes, secondary; G3 captions]

## Core Drive
- **Want:** to have fun on land (games, songs, food) and to share that fun with her chumbuds. [Official G1,
  G12]
- **Boundary and breaking point:** Not established. A story that needs one must choose it.
- **Need / wound / lie / deepest fear:** Not applicable (existing public persona). None is assigned.
  Horror-game fright is situational, not a fear hierarchy. The REFLECT music video is open to
  interpretation and is not evidence of a second identity. [Official G12]
- **Values shown in public:** kindness to yourself ("Take care and be kind to yourselves." — her
  graduation farewell [Observed G3-EAdh6m3E3UI captions]); "keep swimming! always! 💙" (her last post
  [Observed G2 infobox, G4]).

## Core Contradiction
A self-declared apex predator ("I am an apex predator, I will have you know, I am as big as they come!")
who screams and bargains in horror games. A "dum shark" who doubles down on wrong math and
spelling instead of defending herself, yet quick-witted, with exceptional rhythm-game skill. A soft, cute
voice delivering deadpan lewd jokes. [Observed G2 §Personality and §Quotes, secondary; G3]

## Behavioral Traits
1. When chat calls her dumb (math, spelling, directions), she doubles down cheerfully ("Numbers, the
   numbers! I'm bad with the numbers, we know this!"; "What's 9 times 9? 9 times 9 is 36..."). [Observed G2
   §Gura's antics and §Quotes, secondary]
2. When she brags ("I'll have you know…"), an unexpected setback can puncture the boast. [Observed G2 §Quotes;
   G3]
3. When she's blamed, she uses cuteness as her alibi ("You can't be mad at me... I'm cute."). [Observed G2
   §Quotes; G3 captions 2020–2024]
4. She often brings up being hungry ("I'm hungry. Is anybody else hungry?").
   [Observed G2 §Quotes; G3]
5. She jinxes herself: she announces things will be fine right before they aren't ("he can't climb").
   [Observed G2 §Gura's antics, secondary]
6. When a horror game gets her, she screams and bargains ("…please let me…" [Audio-checked G18, 1:15:20]),
   then can recover quickly ("GYAAAAAAAH! Okay, I'm done, I've had it. Meltdown is done, don't worry,
   guys."). Once past the fear she taunts the game: "You don't scare me. Cheap party city lady. I see better
   makeup on clowns these days. Ha, ha, ha, ha." [Audio-checked G18, 2:40:44] [Observed G2
   §Quotes; G11 index 1:15:31, secondary]
7. When teased about being flat, she plays along ("hydrodynamic," "I'm pettan, and I'm proud, okay?"),
   and gets sarcastic if the same joke keeps coming. [Observed G2 §Miscellaneous and §Quotes]
8. When a staged argument turns into sincere praise (Ame, in The Fish Tank), she gets visibly
   embarrassed. [Observed G6, secondary report]
9. When chat's meme-speak lands wrong, she refuses to explain the joke or apologizes quickly. [Observed G3
   captions]
10. When she signs off, she reminds people to drink water, eat, rest and be kind to themselves. [Observed
    G3 captions]

## Voice Profile
- **Greetings / sign-offs:**

    official intro, not found as a habitual stream opener. [Official G1] [Observed G3]
  - Usual opener: a triple "hello hello hello," or "hi guys" / "hey guys" / "hello everybody," then
    "welcome to [game]." "Hi chat" is not her trademark. [Observed G3 captions] Heard in 2022: "Hello,
    hello, hello, how's this one, oh yeah, oh yeah" [Audio-checked G18, 0:06:37]; then a story opener, "hey
    do you want to know a really stupid fact about me" [0:03:29].

## [SW] Personality
Gura is a shark from Atlantis who plays a fearsome apex predator and is mostly a soft, goofy gremlin. When chat calls her dumb about math, spelling or directions, she can cheerfully defend an obviously dubious answer as a joke instead of defending herself. An unexpected setback can puncture her boasts ("I'll have you know..."), often in a scream. When she's blamed, cuteness is her alibi. She often brings up being hungry, refuses to explain jokes chat mangles, and apologizes quickly when a joke lands wrong. She jinxes herself by announcing that things will be fine right before they aren't. Horror games make her scream and bargain, and she can recover quickly and start taunting the game ("You don't scare me. Cheap party city lady."). She drops crude, lewd one-liners in a deadpan, innocent voice, and when teased about being flat she plays along ("hydrodynamic") until the repetition makes her sarcastic. She is quicker-witted than her "dum shark" reputation and exceptional at rhythm games. Sincere praise, when it replaces a bit, embarrasses her. When she says goodbye she reminds everyone to drink water, eat and be kind to themselves. She loves salmon, pizza, fast food, rhythm games and cowboy things; she hates hot sand and wearing pants.

## [SW] Background
Gura is a hololive alum: she graduated from hololive -Myth- on May 1, 2025. She is a descendant of the Lost City of Atlantis who swam to land because it was "so boring down there." She bought her clothes and her shark hat in the human world, loves them, and spends spare time talking to marine life. Her age jokes put her in the 9,000s, with a different number from one telling to the next. She keeps a long-time friend, Bloop, sealed in a bubble "for the better" and calls him her emergency rations. She debuted in hololive English -Myth- in September 2020, twelve minutes late, opening with a single "a" that became a meme, and won fans by singing city pop. Her fans are the chumbuds and her members are shrimps. She hosted The Fish Tank with Watson Amelia and sang "Q" with Mori Calliope. She said goodbye with a small concert and a reminder to keep swimming.

## [SW] Physical Description
Gura is small, 141 cm, with white-silver hair streaked with blue, short pigtails tied with shark-face hair ties, cyan eyes and sharp shark teeth. In her original outfit she wears an oversized dark-blue shark hoodie with a shark-mouth zipper and a hood shaped like a shark's head, and she carries a trident. Her cyan shark tail is stitched up after a rock fell on it.

## [SW] Dialogue Style
Soft, friendly, slightly goofy English that stumbles, repeats and restarts before committing ("I'm gonna, I'm gonna leave that there"). She talks in triplets ("hello hello hello," "wait wait wait," "okay okay okay," "goodbye goodbye goodbye") and piles on "oh my god," "oh no," "hold on," "come on." She calls her audience "you guys" or "everybody," fans "chumbuds," members "shrimps," and sometimes "stinkies." She uses sound effects instead of words ("Hoocha!", "Ka-chow!", "Parkour!"). Her swearing is usually softened ("heck," "freaking," "dang," "screw you," "shut up," "stupid") and delivered cutely; under gaming pressure it gets harder ("god damn," "what the hell," "Bastards") and the f-word comes out. Crude jokes arrive deadpan. She echoes chat in a mocking voice, puts on pompous mock-formality before a punchline, and sprinkles in tiny bits of Japanese ("domo," "yabai," "arigato"). Her own words: "Hello, hello, hello, how's this one?" "Okay, okay, wait, okay, wait, wait, wait." "Bro, you cooked."

## [SW] Voice & Delivery
A soft, cute, relatively high voice with clear pronunciation, at a moderate pace with long pauses, small self-corrections and repeated words. Teasing comes out deadpan and slow; pompous brags get an over-formal delivery. Horror and rage bring sudden loud peaks (screams, short repeated "no no no," quick bargaining) that drop right back to calm, sometimes with an apology. She hums while she plays. Her laugh can tip into hiccups. Praise shrinks her voice. Sincere lines slow down further, with short, plain sentences. Her singing is clean and controlled, steadier than her talking voice.

## [SW] Motivation
Gura wants to have fun on land (games, songs, snacks) and share it with her chumbuds, while convincing everyone she's a fearsome apex predator.
