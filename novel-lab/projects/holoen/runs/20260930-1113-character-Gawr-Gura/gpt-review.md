## Required Changes

1. **Make graduated status explicit in Groups and early in Background.**  
   Replace unqualified “hololive, hololive -Myth-, Myth” with wording such as “hololive -Myth- (graduated), hololive alum.” Preserve the historical branch name in the career history rather than listing it as a current organization. The official profile explicitly labels Gura an alum. [Official Gura profile](https://hololive.hololivepro.com/en/talents/gawr-gura/)

   The project’s continuous-persona rule preserves memories and mannerisms; it does not restore active membership. Amelia’s affiliate status should likewise remain clear where she appears in this card.

2. **Separate the ancient-age performance from a fixed age.**  
   The concept and `[SW] Background` establish “9,000-something” as narrator-certified biography and assert that it changes every time she is asked. Neither universality nor a calendar rule follows from examples of inconsistent answers.

   Use: “Her public age jokes place her in the thousands, with varying numbers across exchanges; no fixed numerical age is adopted.” Attach the supporting performances. Keep the gag available without calculating a birth year or aging it automatically.

3. **Repair corpus provenance and remove unsupported speaking-rate precision.**  
   The introduction claims 31 streams, while G3 identifies 12. Provide the full analyzed manifest or reduce the scope claim. Add timestamps for the principal spoken examples and explain whether rates exclude silence, music, other speakers, and duplicated caption segments.

   Caption words divided by elapsed stream time are not automatically articulation rate. Until the method is established, remove the 105–130 and 90 wpm claims. Preserve qualitative changes in pace only at the level the evidence supports.

4. **Restore explicit, contextual crude language without guessing missing words.**  
   `[SW] Dialogue Style` names mild substitutes but relegates stronger speech to unspecified “harder swears slipping out.” That phrasing implies accidental use and gives Sudowrite little concrete vocabulary. The dossier contains stronger public-comedy leads that should be checked and represented accurately.

   The linked swearing archive identifies a specific *Resident Evil 2* source, making it a useful retrieval lead, but its title alone cannot establish every exact word. Recover the relevant segment or reliable transcription before importing a quotation. [Swearing archive and original-stream reference](https://knowyourmeme.com/videos/300000-gawr-gura)

   Also retain the **ara ara** lead from my draft for verification. If supported, include it as an occasional performed bit. Do not delete it for being provocative, and do not promote it into a habitual seductive register. The goal is supported range, not a stronger or weaker persona chosen by preference.

5. **Remove the rules that make her permanently incompetent.**  
   “Perfect math” in Sounds Off implies that giving a correct answer would be uncharacteristic. The repeated assertions that boasts collapse, hunger fills any lull, and fright resolves within seconds create similarly mechanical behavior.

   Replace these with situational observations: “She can defend an obviously dubious answer as a joke,” and “A boast can be punctured by an unexpected setback.” Keep her capable of competent play, sustained thought, and varied reactions. The invented wrong-arithmetic example should not become the principal calibration line for ordinary speech.

6. **Constrain acoustic and relationship conclusions to what was observed.**  
   A frightened stream title does not establish pitch or scream duration. “One of the loudest … in Myth” requires comparative evidence; “laugh can tip into hiccups” needs a specific source. Mark unsupported delivery directions as interpretations or remove them.

   In `[SW] Relationships`, replace “chaotic best friend” with a public collaboration description. “Best-friend energy” in research does not establish a relationship ranking. Likewise, “protector” should describe the particular public joke or exchange rather than an unconditional duty.

7. **Remove aliases that would predictably identify unrelated subjects.**  
   **Goomba** also names a Mario enemy, and **Apex Predator** is a broad descriptive phrase. Keep these in the research alias register, with their contexts, rather than enabling automatic recognition everywhere. Distinctive alternatives such as Gooba, Goobidiba, and Same-chan remain useful.

   Review **Gawr** separately so automatic matching does not silently settle any alternative-persona interpretation. Do not import the common name “George” simply to make the list exhaustive.

8. **Restore missing structure and claim-level evidence.**  
   The Arc and Secrets sections need their required subfields, even when the answer is “Not established.” Add the boundary/breaking-point entry and label the five scene seeds as unapproved proposals. Use “(None.)” for Intimacy & Boundaries.

   Provide a research-side map identifying official lore, public observation, and unverified claims for card statements, including the tail injury, Bloop details, food preferences, and habitual address terms. Keep one copy of each fixed routine in its most useful field.

## Suggestions

- Keep the distinction between the official profile greeting and ordinary stream openings. Label the former **published profile greeting**, rather than implying “formal intro” is a verified recurring performance category.
- Preserve archival dates while removing phrases that function as restrictions such as “early era only.” Earlier callbacks remain available under the project rule.
- Prefer concrete vocal properties over “childlike”: light register, pauses, altered pronunciations, and sudden changes in intensity can be checked and performed more precisely.
- Retain the warm farewell register near the front of its relevant field. It supplies a useful contrast to teasing and horror reactions.
- Shorten repeated quote blocks. A small set of contextual utterances is more useful than reproducing the same long farewell in several sections.
