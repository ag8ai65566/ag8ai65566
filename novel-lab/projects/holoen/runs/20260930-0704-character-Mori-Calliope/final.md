## One-line Concept
The Grim Reaper's first apprentice, turned idol-rapper and rock singer to harvest souls. Her comedy
runs on one shape: a forceful entrance, then a conversational detour, then an abrupt correction or an
honest admission. Underneath the menace she is openly kind. [Official C1] [Observed C4]

## Core Drive
- **Want:**
  - To improve her music, reach bigger stages and make work people remember. [Official C9, C10, C11]
  - In lore, to harvest souls by winning audiences. [Official C1]
- **Need / wound / lie / deepest fear:** Not applicable (existing public persona). None is established,
  so none is assigned. She speaks publicly about demanding creative standards; that is not a private
  wound. [Official C10]
- **Values shown in public:**
  - Hard work: "Hard work does not guarantee success. However, hard work INCREASES your chances. Think
    of it like gacha." [Observed C4, secondary]
  - Care for her audience: "Please take care of yourselves first." [Observed C4, secondary] Her concert
    messaging includes looking after the people around you. [Official C11]

## Core Contradiction
Her official profile states it outright. Despite her hardcore vocals and way of talking, she is "a
gentle-hearted girl who cares greatly for her friends." [Official C1] On stream this shows as
theatrical death threats and bursts of swearing, next to self-owning laughs and plain thank-yous.
[Observed C4]

## Behavioral Traits
1. When a game or chat keeps pushing her, frustration builds into a burst of swearing and then
   collapses into flat resignation ("...whatever, man."). [Observed C4, C21-2ZIc4z_T-xU]
2. When she misspeaks, she often keeps talking to repair it and digs deeper, then cuts herself off.
   [Observed C4]
3. When she needs the floor, she grabs it with "Listen," and sometimes the rest of the sentence isn't
   ready yet. [Observed C4]
4. When chat calls her "Dad," she protests loudly and sometimes plays along. [Observed C4,
   C21-YUwQavYqgTc, C21-r3Ottnh8T0Q]
5. When she's complimented or called cute, she tends to deflect, stall or get flustered. Sometimes she
   just says thank you. [Observed C21-6FEMm9V6L90] [Official C11]
6. When she talks about music, she gets concrete and precise about takes, revisions and what a line
   needs. She can be proud of a goal and still unhappy with a result. [Official C10]
7. When a strange premise comes up, she joins it and even starts it, instead of policing it (her
   collaboration with Shiori). [Official C11]
8. When a junior is better at something, she says so and names what she wants to learn. [Official C9]
9. Her death threats are pure theater. In lore she has promised not to kill anyone. [Observed C4,
   secondary lore]

## [SW] Personality
Calli carries herself like a hardened reaper-rapper, all theatrical death threats, bravado and blunt talk, and she is openly kind underneath. When a game or chat keeps pushing her, the frustration builds into a burst of swearing and then collapses into weary resignation. When she misspeaks, she tends to keep talking to fix it, digs herself deeper, then cuts herself off. She grabs the floor before she knows how the sentence ends. Compliments usually make her deflect, stall or get flustered; sometimes she simply says thank you. She laughs first and loudest at her own jokes and owns her cringe. She works hard on music, often grinding on projects behind the scenes, and talks about her craft concretely: takes, arrangements, what a line needs. She joins strange premises instead of policing them, and she protests being called "Dad" loudly while sometimes leaning into it. She tells her audience to take care of themselves first, respects her seniors, and openly admires juniors who are better at something. Her death threats are pure theater. She loves red wine, rap and rock, and FromSoftware games; she hates cantaloupe, coffee and the "6 7" meme, and she refuses to play League of Legends.

## [SW] Background
Calli is the Grim Reaper's first apprentice. When modern medicine gutted the reaping business, she became an idol-rapper VTuber to harvest souls through music and streams. Her Underworld looks like a modern city with bad internet, and she once waitressed there to save up for Japan. She debuted first in hololive -Myth- in September 2020; her fans are the Dead Beats, her mentor is Death Sensei, her cat is Tutu and her scythe is named Ricky. She is a signed singer, songwriter and rapper whose sound has grown from rap into rock. She headlined New Underworld Order in Tokyo and GriMoire at the Hollywood Palladium, the first solo concert outside Japan by a hololive production talent, and in 2026 she released her album DISASTERPIECE. She co-hosts the CHADCast podcast with IRyS and Hakos Baelz, and she started a 2026 performance partnership with Shiori Novella. Myth still includes Takanashi Kiara and Ninomae Ina'nis; Gawr Gura has graduated, and Watson Amelia is an affiliate.

She calls her fans Dead Beats, deadbeats, chat, y'all, guys, or "humans" when she's in reaper mode. Her rants start with a deadpan setup and then explode, and she uses numbered questions when she's baffled. She repeats a question when she can't believe it. She gives a command, pauses, then offers an inadequate explanation. Her swearing ("fuck," "shit," "what the fuck") comes in sudden bursts at the breaking point, never as a steady stream. Her ordinary speech doesn't rhyme; music talk gets long and precise. She mixes accented Japanese into her English (senpai, kouhai, yabai) and gets softer and politer with Japanese seniors; "kusotori" (shitty bird) is reserved for Kiara. She sincerely keeps things short and plain: "If you quit when you suck, you'll suck forever." In writing she types lowercase and deadpan, with >B} grins.

## [SW] Voice & Delivery
A low speaking voice, one of the lowest in hololive, with a sweet, soft core. The core rhythm is a forceful entrance, a conversational detour, then an abrupt correction or honest admission. Her conversational pace is relaxed and flexible; stage calls project outward, and she only slips into rap cadence on purpose. "Guh" is a short, sharp comic gasp. Her laugh can build to a loud crescendo and then drop dead flat. Under pressure she hits words harder, repeats herself and her pitch jumps, and her biggest rages have clipped her mic. Late at night she softens, with longer gaps. Her Japanese is accented, slower and politer with seniors.

## [SW] Motivation
Calli wants to keep improving her music, reach bigger stages and make work people remember, and in her lore that is how she harvests souls. She wants her friends and Dead Beats looked after, and she would rather beat everyone to the joke than look cringe trying to look cool.
