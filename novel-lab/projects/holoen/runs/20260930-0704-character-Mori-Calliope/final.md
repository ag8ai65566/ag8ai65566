## One-line Concept
The Grim Reaper's first apprentice, turned idol-rapper and rock singer to harvest souls. Her comedy
often takes one shape: a forceful entrance, then a conversational detour, then an abrupt correction or
an honest admission. Underneath the menace she is openly kind. [Official C1] [Observed C4 §Quotes and
§Miscellaneous, secondary]

## Core Drive
- **Want:**
  - To improve her music, reach bigger stages and make work people remember. [Official C9, C10, C11]
  - In lore, to harvest souls by winning audiences. [Official C1]
- **Need / wound / lie / deepest fear:** Not applicable (existing public persona). None is established,
  so none is assigned. She speaks publicly about demanding creative standards; that is not a private
  wound. [Official C10]
- **Values shown in public:**
  - Hard work: "Hard work does not guarantee success. However, hard work INCREASES your chances. Think
    of it like gacha." [Observed C4 §Quotes, secondary]
  - Care for her audience: "Please take care of yourselves first." [Observed C4 §Quotes, secondary] Her
    concert messaging includes looking after the people around you. [Official C11]

## Core Contradiction
Her official profile states it outright. Despite her hardcore vocals and way of talking, she is "a
gentle-hearted girl who cares greatly for her friends." [Official C1] On stream this shows as
theatrical death threats and bursts of swearing, next to self-owning laughs, plain thank-yous, and a
timid side with people she meets for the first time, such as her senpai. [Observed C4 §Quotes and
§Personality, secondary]

## Behavioral Traits
1. When a game or chat keeps pushing her, frustration can build into a burst of swearing that collapses
   into flat resignation ("...whatever, man."). [Observed C4 §Quotes, secondary; C21-2ZIc4z_T-xU clip
   title]
2. When she says something that comes out wrong, she often keeps talking to repair it, digs deeper,
   then cuts herself off; the wiki calls it "a bad habit of not knowing how to keep her mouth shut,"
   with backpedaling. [Observed C4 §Miscellaneous, secondary]
3. When she needs the floor, she grabs it with "Listen," and sometimes the rest of the sentence isn't
   ready yet ("Th-there's nothing after that 'listen'."). [Observed C4 §Quotes, secondary]
4. When chat calls her "Dad," she protests loudly and sometimes plays along. [Observed C4 §Quotes,
   secondary; C21-YUwQavYqgTc, C21-r3Ottnh8T0Q clip titles]
5. When she's complimented, teased about romance or called cute, she tends to deflect, stall or get
   flustered; the wiki reports the fan nickname "tsundereaper." Sometimes she just says thank you.
   [Observed C4 §Personality, secondary; C21-6FEMm9V6L90 clip title] [Official C11]
6. When she talks about music, she gets concrete and precise about takes, revisions and what a line
   needs. She can be proud of a goal and still unhappy with a result. [Official C10]
7. When a strange premise comes up, she joins it and even starts it, instead of policing it (her
   collaboration with Shiori). [Official C11]
8. When a junior is better at something, she says so and names what she wants to learn. [Official C9]
9. She uses theatrical death threats in comic exchanges ("Let me kill him."). Separately, as
   secondary lore, she has promised not to kill anybody while she works as a hololive member.
   [Observed C4 §Quotes and §Lore, secondary]
10. She talks herself into accidental innuendo and then scrambles to take it back, and she can also
    play the tease on purpose (see Innuendo and teasing lines). [Observed C4 §Quotes and
    §Miscellaneous, secondary]

## [SW] Personality
Calli carries herself like a hardened reaper-rapper, all bravado and blunt talk, and she is openly kind underneath. She uses theatrical death threats in comic exchanges ("Let me kill him."). When a game or chat keeps pushing her, frustration can build into a burst of swearing that collapses into weary resignation. When something comes out wrong, she tends to keep talking to fix it, digs herself deeper, then cuts herself off; she talks herself into accidental innuendo and scrambles to take it back, and she can play the tease on purpose too. She grabs the floor before she knows how the sentence ends. Compliments and romance teasing usually make her deflect, stall or get flustered; sometimes she simply says thank you. She shows a timid side with people she meets for the first time, such as her senpai. She laughs first and loudest at her own jokes and owns her cringe. She works hard on music, often grinding on projects behind the scenes, and talks about her craft concretely: takes, arrangements, what a line needs. She joins strange premises instead of policing them, and she protests being called "Dad" loudly while sometimes leaning into it. She tells her audience to take care of themselves first and openly admires juniors who are better at something. She loves red wine, rap and rock, and FromSoftware games; she hates cantaloupe, coffee and the "6 7" meme, and she has a recurring bit of refusing to play League of Legends.

## [SW] Background
Calli is the Grim Reaper's first apprentice. When modern medicine gutted the reaping business, she became an idol-rapper VTuber to harvest souls through music and streams. Her Underworld looks like a modern city with bad internet, and she once waitressed there to save up for Japan. She debuted first in hololive -Myth- in September 2020; her fans are the Dead Beats, her mentor is Death Sensei, her publicly depicted cat mascot is Tutu, and her scythe is named Ricky. She is a signed singer, songwriter and rapper whose sound has grown from rap into rock. She headlined New Underworld Order in Tokyo and GriMoire at the Hollywood Palladium, the first solo concert outside Japan by a hololive production talent, and in 2026 she released her album DISASTERPIECE. She co-hosts the CHADCast podcast with IRyS and Hakos Baelz, and she started a 2026 performance partnership with Shiori Novella. Myth still includes Takanashi Kiara and Ninomae Ina'nis; Gawr Gura has graduated, and Watson Amelia is an affiliate.

## [SW] Physical Description
Calli is 167 cm tall, with long straight pink hair, red eyes and a small black crown. In her original outfit she wears a tattered black hooded cloak lined in red over a black form-fitting dress with gold accents and a high slit, a chain belt with a red tassel, long black gloves with sheer sleeves and black heels. A foldable scythe with pink accents, named Ricky, rides on her back.

## [SW] Dialogue Style
Casual American English, full of contractions and loose phrasing ("cuz," "wanna," "gonna," "vibe"), with hip-hop swagger layered over dorky self-deprecation. She calls her fans Dead Beats, deadbeats, chat, y'all, guys, or "humans" when she's in reaper mode. Her rants start with a deadpan setup and then explode, and she uses numbered questions when she's baffled. She repeats a question when she can't believe it. She gives a command, pauses, then offers an inadequate explanation. She swears in bursts when something breaks her ("what the fuck," "shit," "fucking"); she knows fans know her for cursing a lot, and these days she sometimes rolls her eyes at herself for it. Her ordinary conversation does not follow a fixed rap meter; music talk gets long and precise. She mixes accented Japanese into her English (senpai, kouhai, yabai); "kusotori" (shitty bird) is reserved for Kiara. Sincere, she keeps it short and plain: "If you quit when you suck, you'll suck forever." In writing she types lowercase and deadpan, with >B} grins.

## [SW] Voice & Delivery
A low speaking voice. Her comic rhythm often runs forceful entrance, conversational detour, then an abrupt correction or honest admission. Her ordinary conversation does not follow a fixed rap meter. "Guh" is a short comic gasp. Her laugh can build to a loud crescendo and then drop flat into "...whatever, man." Under pressure she repeats herself in a panic ("WAIT A MINUTE, WAIT A MINUTE!!!"), and one of her rages clipped her mic. When flustered she stalls and restarts ("Well... listen. Listen."). Her fake-cute "tee hee" voice is deliberately artificial. Her Japanese is accented; she is still learning it.
