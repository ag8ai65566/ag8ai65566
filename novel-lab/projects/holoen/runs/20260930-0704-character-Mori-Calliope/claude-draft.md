# Character File: Mori Calliope

> Scope: official lore + publicly shown on-stream/on-social persona only, as of 2026-09-30.
> Nothing about the performer behind the avatar. Verbatim quotes are marked "verbatim";
> lines written by us to show the style are marked "style demo". Source numbers → Sources.

## One-line concept
The Grim Reaper's first apprentice, who took up idol-rapping to harvest souls after modern medicine
wrecked the reaping business, and turned out to be a husky-voiced, swearing, workaholic softie
who would rather die (again) than admit she cares this much. [1][2]

## Core drive
- **Want (surface goal):** keep "harvesting souls" — i.e. win over audiences with music and streams;
  keep leveling up as an artist (rap → rock, bigger stages). [1][3][14]
- **Need (persona reading, not fact):** permission to rest and to be openly soft without the
  reaper/rapper bravado as cover. Based on the on-stream workaholic and tsundere bits. [2]
- **Wound:** none established in official lore. Do not invent one. The usable pressure point is
  the workaholic streak (famous for going long stretches without sleep). [2]
- **Lie she performs (persona bit):** that being soft ruins the reaper image — so every sincere
  moment gets undercut with a joke, a "LISTEN", or a death threat she will never carry out. [2]
- **Fears (on-stream):** looking cringe while trying to look cool (then owning it: "Cringe is
  like, my brand" — verbatim [2]); crowds; hospitals. [2]
- **Line she won't cross:** lore — she has promised not to kill anyone; her menace is
  theatrical. [2] Running bit — she will not play League of Legends. [2]

## Core contradiction
Hardcore reaper-rapper voice, profanity and death jokes on the outside; per the official
profile, "a gentle-hearted girl who cares greatly for her friends" underneath (verbatim [1]).
She swings between smug confidence and panic within one sentence, and is the first to laugh
at her own bad joke. [1][20]

## Behavioral traits
1. When complimented or called cute, she deflects, stammers, pretends not to hear, or starts
   "LISTEN—" and never finishes the sentence. [2][24]
2. When she misspeaks, she keeps talking to fix it and digs the hole deeper, then tells
   herself to shut up. [2][24]
3. When she loses or chat backseats, the volume climbs into a burst of swearing, then she
   deflates into a flat "...whatever, man." [2]
4. When chat bullies or "gaslights" her, she plays the wounded victim — then starts banning
   the offending meme (e.g. "...", "6 7"). [24]
5. When a friend is hurting, she turns protective and almost parental, and tells fans to take
   care of themselves first. [2][24]
6. When she has a drink on stream, the first sip gets a sharp, satisfied "Guh." [2][20]
7. When she talks music, she switches to focused, technical, self-critical mode and talks
   about grinding. [23]

- **Vocabulary / slang / fillers / profanity:** casual American English with hip-hop slang
  ("big ups", "your boy", "y'all"); "chat", "guys", "humans" (persona voice). Profanity: heavy
  but in **bursts** when tilted or excited — not a constant stream; she has said cursing now
  makes her roll her eyes at herself. [2][20]
- **Interjections, laughs, noises:** "Guh."; laugh that builds to a crescendo and drops flat
  ("hahahaHAHAHA... whatever, man."); panicked repeats ("WAIT A MINUTE, WAIT A MINUTE!!!"). [2]
- **Code-switching:** conversational Japanese with an audible accent; uses senpai / kouhai /
  yabai; bilingual stream titles; once wrote 僕 (boku, boyish "I") in a title — habit unverified.
  In Japanese she gets slower, softer and more polite. [2][15][22][24]
- **How she addresses others:** fans = "Dead Beats"/"deadbeats", "chat", "y'all";
  Japanese seniors = "-senpai"; juniors = "kouhai"; Kiara = formerly "kusotori" (teasing);
  Bijou = "Biboo". Says her own name Western-order, "Calliope Mori"; prefers "Calli". [2][24][25]
- **Rhythm & rhetoric:** deadpan setup → explosion; backpedalling; smug "business lady" rhyming
  when confident; theatrical death threats ("Let me kill him." — verbatim [2]); sincerity framed
  as gaming advice ("If you quit when you suck, you'll suck forever." — verbatim [2]).
- **Timbre / pitch / pace / accent (for voice performance):** one of the lowest speaking voices
  in hololive — husky, low alto with a sweet, soft core; relaxed default pace; rap cadence when
  hyped; rages can peak hard enough to clip the mic. [2][24]
- **Would never say / sounds off:** constant high-pitched idol cutesiness or "uwu"; flawless
  native-level Japanese; genuinely cruel lines to fans; smooth confidence with zero self-own;
  knowing every new slang term; happily agreeing to play League; liking cantaloupe or the
  "6 7" meme. [2][24]

## Arc (hooks for fan fiction)
- Start: the reaper who swears she's here to work, not to make friends.
- Turning points: a friend needs her and the bravado drops; a music deadline pushes the
  workaholic streak too far; someone makes her say something sincere without a joke after it.
- End (open): she admits, out loud and without "LISTEN", that the Dead Beats and her friends matter.

  spiral of explanations; theatrical threats that others take literally; refuses things on principle

## [SW] Personality
Calli acts like a hardened reaper-rapper and is a softie underneath. When complimented or called cute, she deflects, stammers, or starts "LISTEN—" and never finishes. When she misspeaks, she keeps explaining and digs the hole deeper, then tells herself to shut up. When she loses, her volume climbs into a burst of swearing and then collapses into a flat "...whatever, man." She laughs first and loudest at her own bad jokes and owns her cringe ("Cringe is like, my brand"). She is a workaholic who skips sleep for music and treats hard work like gacha: more pulls, better odds. When a friend is hurting, the bravado drops and she turns protective and parental, telling people to take care of themselves first. Her death threats are pure theater; she has promised not to kill anyone. She refuses to play League of Legends, hates cantaloupe and the "6 7" meme, and loves red wine, FromSoftware games, rap, rock, and anime like JoJo and Gachiakuta.

## [SW] Background
Calli is the Grim Reaper's first apprentice, from an Underworld that looks like a modern city with bad internet, where she once waitressed to save up for Japan. When modern medicine gutted the reaping business, she became an idol-rapper VTuber to harvest souls through music and streams; souls "vaporized" by wholesome moments flow to her too. She debuted first in hololive -Myth- in September 2020, and her fans are the Dead Beats. She is now a signed recording artist who has headlined overseas concerts and sung anime openings, and her sound has moved from rap toward rock. Her mentor is Death Sensei; her cat is Tutu; her scythe is named Ricky. Since the 2026 merger, her unit is hololive -Myth-, and she wants to do more for Japanese audiences.

## [SW] Voice & Delivery
One of the lowest speaking voices in hololive: a husky low alto with a sweet, soft core. Her default pace is relaxed and a little mumbly, and it snaps into rap cadence when she is hyped. "Guh." is a sharp, satisfied gasp. Her laugh builds to a loud crescendo and then drops dead flat. Rage spikes hard enough to clip the mic, and panic comes out as rapid repeats ("WAIT A MINUTE, WAIT A MINUTE!"). When flustered she stammers and her pitch jumps. When sincere she goes slow, quiet, and low. Late at night she turns drowsy and drifts off mid-thought. Her Japanese is slower and politer, with an audible accent.
