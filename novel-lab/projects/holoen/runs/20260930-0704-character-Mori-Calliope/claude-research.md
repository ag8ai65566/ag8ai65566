## 1. Status & basics

  brand [5][6][7]; her unit is officially "hololive -Myth-" [1][2]. At the announcement she wanted more
  initiatives for a Japanese audience [6].

  "hey deadbeats, update: … things are looking up and I'm doing alright… I'll be back soon enough" [8];
  resumed ~2026-05-16 and asked fans not to attack management [10] (exact date unverified).

  Calli, Ina, Kiara remain [4][14].
- Debut 2020-09-12, first of Myth ("Memento Mori! What is Up, Humans?!") [2]. Birthday April 4 (4 = "shi" =
  death pun) [2]. Height 167 cm [1]. Illustrator Yukisame [1].
- Fan name **Dead Beats** ("Deadbeats", デッドビーツ), picked over "Calliopeople" [2]. Fan mark 💀 [2][23].
  Mascot "Death Sensei" (chibi Grim Reaper mentor, designed by Ina); cat "Tutu" (emote + model toggle) [2].

- Lore: profile opens "Dead Beats, listen up! Your Mori's been working hard for you!" [1]. Grim Reaper's
  first apprentice; modern medicine hurt reaping, so she became a VTuber to harvest souls [1][2]; souls
  "vaporized" by wholesome VTuber moments also go to her [2]. Despite hardcore vocals/speech she is "a
  gentle-hearted girl who cares greatly for her friends" [1]; 2020 profile: sweet voice contradicts morbid
  talk [2]. Scythe "Ricky" (stainless steel, summonable; named after Kiara's "Kikkeriki") [2][24:_QM8QYYYFnE].
  Underworld = modern city with bad internet; waitressed there to save for Japan; surprised her human body
  needs sleep; promised not to kill anyone; true name unpronounceable [2].
- Design: long straight pink hair, small black crown; black form-fitting dress, gold accents, high slit,
  red lining; tattered black hooded cloak (red inside); chain belt with red tassel; long black gloves with
  sheer sleeves; black heels with gold; foldable scythe with pink accents on her back; palette
  black/gold/pink/red [2].

## 2. Voice
### Greetings / sign-offs
- "What is up, humans?!" — debut title & early tweet opener [2][23]; Hololive Fandom caption [19].
- "Hey deadbeats" / "Dead Beats, listen up!" — lowercase in tweets [8], profile line [1].
- "Guh" (ンガッ) at stream start as well as after drinks — JP fan blogs (unverified) [22].
- Sign-off "I'm your Mori, and I hope you'll remember me" (memento mori pun) [2]; current frequency unverified.
- Unverified/unsupported: "Moshi moshi" (appears in a lyric, not a documented opener); "Nice to reap you"
  (search claim only); "MI~NASAN" (JP unofficial wiki).
- Written voice: mostly lowercase, deadpan, "y'all" [8]; kaomoji and "B}"/"8}" grins ("I'll keep grinding!
  >B}", "Sending Off MORE Supers to the Underworld! 8}") [23][24]; one-word joke titles ("fih", "golly",
  "hungry for wall chicken", "we're back, baby", "...slap it?", "gigi made the thumbnail") [15].

### Catchphrases & running bits (verified)
- **"Guh."** #1 catchphrase: comic gasp after a drink (often wine); "like a downplayed Beergasm" [20];
  own emote and a song titled "guh" [2][20][21]. Imitated by Mococo, Mumei [24:3u-W65jx38U, l6VZl2-uE2Y].
  Some drinking streams used non-alcoholic wine [24:qYk8v9iT4vM].
- **"LISTEN." / "Well… listen. Listen."** — defensive stall before an argument/excuse; gag that nothing
  follows ("Th-there's nothing after that 'listen'") [2].
- **"Your boy."** self-reference; the only EN member with a near-equivalent of a quirky JP pronoun [2].
  Alter egos "C-Man", "YUNG SH1N1GAM1 B01" [2].
- **"Dad" / "I AM NOT YOUR DAD!"** — from "Gura's dad" joke; protests loudly [2][24:YUwQavYqgTc] but leaned in
  ("Hello, Dead Beats. I'm Dad. Was That Okay?" [24:r3Ottnh8T0Q]); "Dad" to Kobo [25]. TV Tropes snippet:
  "Dad is sad, and now we know why!" [20].
- **"Cringe is like, my brand."** [2]

- **"Big ups."** ("The BIGGEST of 'Ups'…" [23]; explained in Japanese [24:xl1BDeHKxqI]).
- **"EN's Law"** — more people in a collab = more scuff [2].

## 5. Impersonator checklist
- Get right: low husky voice with soft core; "Guh" after sips; fan name as vocative; profanity in bursts that
  deflate into "whatever, man."; "listen" stall; "your boy"/"Dad"; "Big ups"; self-owning laughs; workaholic
  music talk; accented Japanese that gets softer/politer with seniors; lowercase deadpan written voice with >B}.
- Off: constant high idol cuteness/"uwu"; fluent native Japanese; cruelty toward fans; steady swearing; smooth
  confidence with no self-deprecation; knowing all slang; happily playing League; liking cantaloupe or "67";
  truly malicious reaper.
