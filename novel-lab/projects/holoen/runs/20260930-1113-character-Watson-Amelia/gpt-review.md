## Required Changes

1. **Do not treat reconstructed profanity or combined quotations as verbatim.**  
   The opening legend explicitly says uncensored wording was inferred. The card nevertheless presents a complete rage sequence as exact dialogue. Recover the recording or a reliable uncensored transcription; until then, mark the full wording unverified in research.

   Sample Line 3 is also a composite, and its “I’m not cheating” differs from the listed “It’s not cheating.” Separate the quotations or label the assembled line a style example. The authenticity rule requires uncensored verified words; it does not authorize filling evidential gaps.

   The ground-pound reference itself contains different longer transcriptions in its introductory and origin paragraphs. Preserve the stable joke and game-text attribution without pretending that secondary wording settles the complete audio. [Ground-pound origin account](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/nothing-beats-a-ground-pound)

2. **Correct the Ina relationship sentence without sanitizing the joke.**  
   `[SW] Relationships` says Amelia “promised to get her ‘fucked’ in a PvP game.” That is not an accurate paraphrase of addressing someone with a competitive defeat taunt.

   Replace it with: “Ninomae Ina’nis — Myth colleague and gaming collaborator; Amelia can direct blunt competitive taunts at her.” If the exact profane wording is verified, retain it as a gaming quotation with its context. Do not convert it into a promise about sexual activity.

3. **Keep the time-travel premise while qualifying unsupported mechanics.**  
   `[SW] Background` treats a 1920s birth, involuntary displacement, a time crystal, headaches, and a specific travel sound as settled facts. A general wiki citation does not establish the original statement, its seriousness, or its continuity scope.

   Retain the documented time-travel identity. The official game explicitly presents travel through past and future, but does not thereby establish the proposed birth history or universal costs. Move the additional claims to research as secondary-reported lore pending source verification. [Official ChikuTaku game page](https://watsonamelia.itch.io/chikutaku)

   “Keep her detective agency open” likewise needs a source or an adaptation label; the official detective premise alone does not establish a business-survival objective.

4. **Correct the ChikuTaku dates and production credit.**  
   The timeline’s single January 17, 2023 entry combines song and game release. The official music listing gives **January 19, 2023** as its on-sale date; the game page lists **January 24, 2023, 02:30 UTC**. Separate the events. If January 17 refers to a reveal or performance, name that event and source it separately. [Official song listing](https://hololive.hololivepro.com/en/music/254/), [game release information](https://watsonamelia.itch.io/chikutaku)

   Replace “made her own rhythm game” with her credited role: concept and shared project management, working with a credited developer, artists, and other contributors. Also substantiate the hololive-wide “first charity stream” superlative or reduce it to the specific documented charity event.

5. **Make affiliate status explicit and avoid implying a standing schedule.**  
   Use Groups such as “hololive (affiliate), hololive -Myth- (affiliate).” Put the September 30, 2024 transition early in Background. The official notice distinguishes the conclusion of regular activities from continued affiliation and says future activities will be announced individually. [COVER notice](https://cover-corp.com/en/news/detail/20240920-01)

   Confirm each cameo’s event, date, and segment, then describe it as a completed appearance. “She still turns up now and then” is less precise and can imply an ongoing commitment.

   Remove “any explanation of her 2024 status change” from Sounds Off. The dossier must explain the public status accurately; what remains outside scope is an invented private explanation.

6. **Replace universal gremlin behavior with context-dependent actions.**  
   “Turns any innocent line,” “refuses every hint,” “fixes everyone’s tech,” and the automatic rage-to-apology sequence exceed the evidence. Replace them with bounded behaviors: she can insist on a chosen method, redirect a setup into a crude joke, help with technical problems, or deflate a heated reaction.

   The Story Engine’s detective who ignores every clue and solves the case anyway also undermines the stated investigative premise. A better proposed conflict is that she rejects a suggested solution because it leaves one clue unexplained, then has to test her alternative.

   “Closest banter partner” requires comparative evidence. Use a documented collaboration pattern with Gura instead of ranking relationships.

7. **Separate vocal evidence, metadata, and performance interpretation.**  
   Clip titles cannot establish phonetic “ahkay,” breathiness, register, or the exact quality of a screech. The figurative wiki description may remain attributed secondary reception, but it should not masquerade as acoustic analysis.

   Verify representative ordinary, frustrated, sincere, and announcer passages. Until then, mark unsupported directions as provisional. Preserve the British voice as a performed bit; remove the assertion that it is *always* obviously performed.

   Change “No fixed branded sign-off exists” to “No fixed branded sign-off was established in the reviewed material.” Lack of a finding is not proof of nonexistence.

8. **Complete the structure and distinguish evidence categories.**  
   Restore the boundary/breaking-point entry, Arc subfields, and the five Secrets/Foreshadowing subfields. Add the missing ordinary and tired delivery coverage, explicitly marked unverified where necessary. Label scene seeds as unapproved fiction and use “(None.)” for Intimacy & Boundaries.
