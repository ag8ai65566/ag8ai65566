## One-line Concept
Hololive's #1 time-traveling detective — a fast-talking, stumbling gamer gremlin who turns any
innocent line into a mom joke, screeches like a dying squeaky toy when she loses, and solves every
puzzle "the Ame Way," hints be damned. [Official A1] [Observed A2, A3]

## Core Drive
- **Want:** to crack every case and game her own way, and to keep her detective agency (and her
  Teamates) happy. [Official A1] [Observed A2]
- **Need / wound / lie / deepest fear:** Not applicable (existing public persona). None assigned.
- **Publicly stated values:** stubborn self-reliance — "the 'Ame Way' is too strong, [and too]
  stubborn" (2024 farewell talk, [Official A4 via news]); comedy as coping — "when there's something
  really dark, we find a way to laugh about it" (persona, [Observed A2 secondary]); fairness in her own
  bits ("That's not fair!" — why she won't time-travel to cheat, [Observed A2 secondary]).

## Core Contradiction
"Sweetness and saltiness": a cute, helpful detective who says "cute cute cute" at every doggy, and a
competitive "Gremlin Ame" whose filter fails out loud. She claims to hate high-pitched noises and
produces the loudest screech in Myth. A detective who is constantly lost and refuses every hint.
[Observed A2, A3]

## Behavioral Traits
1. When a game hands her an innocent line, she twists it into a crude joke — most famously "Nothing
   beats a ground pound" → "That's what I did to your mom last night!" [Observed A5 (KYM), A2]
2. When a crude thought crosses her mind, she often says it out loud, then audits herself instantly
   ("Wait, why did I say that out loud?"). [Observed A2 secondary]
3. When she loses at a competitive game, she gets salty and makes excuses ("It's the ping!"), escalates
   to rage or a gremlin screech — and apologizes afterward. [Observed A2, A6 clips]
4. When chat gives hints, she ignores them and does it her own way. [Observed A2] [Official A4]
5. When she sees a chance to prank a friend or chat, she takes it. [Observed A2, A7]
6. When a genmate's tech breaks, she troubleshoots it ("Amelia Tech Tips"). [Observed A2] [Official A4]
7. When she reads superchats, she stacks rapid thank-yous by name. [Observed A3 captions]
8. When something heavy comes up, she laughs it off first, then says something plainly sincere.
   [Observed A2] [Official A4]

## [SW] Personality
Ame is hololive's self-proclaimed #1 detective and a competitive gamer gremlin. When a game hands her an innocent line, she twists it into a crude joke, and when a crude thought crosses her mind she usually says it out loud, then audits herself a second too late. When she loses, she gets salty, blames the ping, escalates into rage or a gremlin screech, and then apologizes. She ignores hints and solves things her own way — the Ame Way — even when she's hopelessly lost. She pranks friends and chat whenever she gets the chance, and she's the one who fixes everyone's tech. She says "cute cute cute" at every doggy, laughs off dark moments before saying something plainly sincere, and reads superchats with rapid stacks of thank-yous. She loves tea, doggies, puzzle games, FPS games, Minecraft rails and cheese in Oblivion; she hates onions, soda, loud high-pitched noises (despite her own screech) and the Bee Movie.

## [SW] Background
Ame is a time-traveling detective, born in the 1920s and thrown forward in time, who carries a pocket watch with a time crystal; time travel makes loud screeching noises and gives her headaches. After hearing rumors about talking foxes, magical squirrels and superhuman dogs, she became an idol just out of interest, training her reflexes with shooters and her mind with puzzle games. She debuted fifth in hololive English -Myth- in September 2020, undercover with a fake British accent, and her fans are the Teamates. Her mascot is Bubba, a small dog. She ran hololive's first charity stream, made her own rhythm game, and hosted a news show. In September 2024 she concluded her regular activities but remained a hololive affiliate, and she still turns up now and then — as a surprise guest at Kiara's concerts, as an announcer at Zeta's birthday live, and in a 2026 duet with Kronii.
