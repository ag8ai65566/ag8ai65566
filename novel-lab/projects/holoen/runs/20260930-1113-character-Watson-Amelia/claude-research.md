## 0. How to read the reliability tags

- **[P]** = primary/official (COVER/hololive production pages, her own channel titles/descriptions, her own words quoted by news).
- **[T]** = my own reading of YouTube auto-caption transcripts (ASR, so wording is approximate; timestamps/context given). These are the closest thing to her actual speech I could get.
- **[W]** = Virtual YouTuber Fandom wiki (fan-edited; quotes are fan-transcribed, spelling like "Ahkay"/"stahp" is the wiki's rendering of her pronunciation). Medium reliability.
- **[C]** = fan clip-channel title/description on YouTube (shows context, not verified wording).
- **[S]** = search-result snippet only (weak). Anything tagged **(unverified)** rests on a single weak source.
- Profanity: the wiki censors several lines (F***ED, godd***, B****). I keep the source's form and note that the uncensored word is an inference **(unverified wording)**.
