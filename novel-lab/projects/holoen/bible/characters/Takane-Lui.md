## One-line Concept
"Did I Luive you waiting!?": holoX's executive officer and point of contact, a hawk with a calm big-sister manner
who handles what the founder cannot, cares for her subordinates, makes dad jokes, screams at horror games,
predicts every G1 horse race, and "pons" (blunders) at the crucial moment. [Official LU1] [Observed LU2
§Personality, secondary] [ASR LU20]

## [SW] Personality
Lui is the executive officer of Secret Society holoX and, per her official profile, its point of contact, who handles what the founder, La+ Darknesss, cannot. She looks cool and aloof, but she is a warm, motherly big sister who cares for her "subordinates"; she reins in La+ and Chloe and reaches out to overseas colleagues and viewers. Her profile also makes her holoX's airhead: "PON" is the meme for her blunders, and fans tell her not to drop her water. She is a hawk and one of hololive's birds, calm and conversational, and she can sharpen a line into a cool executive tone before undercutting it with a sparkle (「コッ☆」, "Ko!☆"). She warns that she is not good with horror ("beware of screams"), plays RPGs on Saturdays, predicts nearly every G1 horse race and loves twins; she has been a Code Geass ambassador since 2025. On stream she reassures chat: 「まあ誰にだってトラブルやミスはあるからね」 ("well, everyone has trouble and mistakes").

## [SW] Background
Lui is an active member of Secret Society holoX. She has no supernatural abilities; her lore is a performed persona. She debuted on 2021-11-27 as the second member of Secret Society holoX, hololive's sixth Japanese generation, and belongs to the bird unit HOLOTORI, whose 2023 lineup was Lui, Takanashi Kiara, Oozora Subaru, Pavolia Reine and Nanashi Mumei. She released the album "Liberty" (2024), the EP "Lieblings" (2025) and the EP "The LEGENDARY" with "Soar" (2026-06), and performed at holoX's first in-person unit concert, "First MISSION," at Pia Arena MM (2026-04-29). In June 2026 COVER announced her BAYFM78 radio programme and her 1st live, "REBELLION," set for 2026-12-16 at Kanadevia Hall. With the English cast: English practice with Mori Calliope (2021), Calli's lesson #04 with Chloe (2022), Calli's "HOLOYOI" episode 1 with Chloe (2023), a Wario off-collab with Kiara (2023), "TWIN DAY WITH LUI" with FUWAMOCO (2023), Hakos Baelz's "BAE-GEMITE DOMINATION" episode 5 with Chloe (2023) and "Q&A With Bird Sisters" with Mumei (2025). Several English members' channels posted animated "Soar" shorts (2026).
