### hololive GAMERS — `bible/world/hololive-GAMERS.md`
**[SW] Other Names:** GAMERS, ゲマズ, ホロライブゲーマーズ
**[SW] Description:** hololive GAMERS is hololive's gaming unit of four animal-eared girls, introduced with a unit video in December 2018. Shirakami Fubuki, a white-haired otaku fox who also belongs to the 1st generation, loves talking with people and wants to be a top otaku idol; Ookami Mio, a black-haired wolf girl who turned up from nowhere and haunts shrines, debuted on 2018-12-07; Nekomata Okayu, a relaxed, boyish cat, and Inugami Korone, a dog who minds a city bakery and games in her free time, debuted in April 2019. Together they ran a radio show in 2019, panic through ghost hunts and party games, and hold fan festivals: "超超超超ゲーマーズ" at Yoyogi National Gymnasium in May 2024 and "超超超超ゲーマーズ2" at Saitama Super Arena on 2025-07-05 and 07-06. They sing "To Be Continued…." and "We are GAMERS !!!!", and Fubuki's "ワンダーフューチャー！" features the other three (2025). Inside the unit: FubuMio (Fubuki and Mio), OkaKoro (Okayu and Korone), Mio and Korone's "ふたりは神様MaxDOG" and Okayu and Fubuki's NYANGUCORN covers. Outside it: Fubuki and Mio sing with Nakiri Ayame as AyaFubuMi, Korone and Ayame are the "Onigashima combo," Fubuki and Houshou Marine are BABACORN, and Korone is one of the Yakamashi Musume with Marine, Shirogane Noel and Yukihana Lamy. With the English cast, Korone played a bilingual bomb-defusal game with Mori Calliope (2020) and Mio held a mochi-pounding off-collab with FUWAMOCO (2025).
**[SW] Rules:** The animal personas (fox, wolf, cat, dog) are performed lore; the members are streamers. Fubuki belongs to both the 1st generation and GAMERS. Pair names describe collab series, not exclusive relationships; collab titles show that a collab happened, not how close two members are. Festival guest lists are dated.
