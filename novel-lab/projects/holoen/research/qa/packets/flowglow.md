### FLOW GLOW — `bible/world/FLOW-GLOW.md`
**[SW] Other Names:** FG, フロウグロウ
**[SW] Description:** FLOW GLOW is a five-member music unit that debuted on 2024-11-09 through hololive DEV_IS, which COVER restructured in 2026. Officially their skills are unpolished and their trust incomplete, but they acknowledge each other's strengths. Isaki Riona is the stoic, competitive leader who melts for small animals; Koganei Niko is the jokester who will break character for a laugh; Mizumiya Su is the tiny publicity manager who wants love and praise; Rindo Chihaya is the DJ and self-proclaimed 300-horsepower driver; Kikirara Vivi is the makeup artist who hunts beauty. In their lore they hang out at NOW RAWDING, a rented former café crowded with everyone's things, and travel in Fugutarō, Chihaya's huge customized car with an opening roof. Their debut song "FG ROADSTER" gives every member a rap verse. They held a 3D debut live, "MAKE IT, BREAK IT," on 2025-11-23, released the album "FLOW GLOW" on 2026-01-21, appeared at hololive 7th fes. in 2026, released "magic summer" and solo singles for all five in 2026, and marked their second anniversary with "BOUNCE BACK！" (2026-11-10). Inside the unit they pair off for covers and collabs: Riona and Niko ("Gimme×Gimme," "逆様"), Su and Chihaya ("レイメイ"), Su and Vivi ("クリームで会いにいけますか"), Vivi and Chihaya ("Bridal Dream"); Chihaya, Riona and Niko call themselves "team hentai gentlemen." With the English cast: Riona calls Calli her "beloved Calli-senpai" (a Clubhouse Games collab, 2026); Niko sang LYRA's "III" with Calli and Elizabeth and "Magia" with Calli and Nerissa; Calli, Nerissa, IRyS and Bijou danced to Riona's "Ring my name"; FUWAMOCO and Bijou watched their debut. With Japanese seniors: AZKi (RoARiS, a 3D karaoke with Koyori, Riona and Niko, and guest spots for Riona and Su at her 2026 birthday live), Lui, Okayu, Marine, Lamy and Chloe.

Collab titles show that a collab happened, not how close two members are; cover partners are listed per song. Pre-debut life and the performers' private lives are outside the cards.
