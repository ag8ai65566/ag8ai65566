# 二波打法 · 五步三開關

His own words, from the 2026-08-10 transcript (`MrnjBdgQPLU`). Transcript-backed —
no longer reconstructed. His framing: *"名詞可以隨便換，但五步跟三個開關一個字都不用改。"*

The playbook does not call the top. It answers three questions only:
**現在我們在哪？油還夠不夠？逃生鈴響了沒？**

---

## 五步

### 第一步 · 定位（看價格 + 籌碼結構）
After a first wave that roughly doubles, look for **高位寬幅橫盤** with **插針**（wicks）,
**縮量**, and **波動率放大**. If you see that pattern, pin yourself to the coordinate
"we are in a shakeout zone" and treat every 鬼故事 as background noise.

### 第二步 · 計量（去產業鏈最上游找金絲雀）
Go to the **top** of the supply chain, not the headline names. Read **訂單、預付款、
照付不易協議 (take-or-pay)** — hard contract structures, real money.

> This reading only answers **"is the mania still on?"** It never answers **"where is
> the top?"** The more glorious the upstream looks, the deeper into the endgame the
> positioning game already is.

His worked example: Micron's FY26 Q3 (2026-06-24) — revenue $41.46B (+346% YoY), gross
margin 85% (from 39%), net income $28.2B, capex raised to $27B, and **16 strategic
customer agreements locking >$22B of commitments with $18B of cash prepayments**, on
five-year **irrevocable take-or-pay** terms.

### 第三步 · 換決策變數（絕對值 → 導數）
Switch the decision variable from **利潤絕對值** to **增速的導數**: the **QoQ** growth
rate of downstream hyperscaler capex — *not* YoY.

> **思科悖論.** Cisco's revenue did **not** collapse when its stock did: ~$19B in
> 2000 at the bubble peak, rising to ~$22B in 2001 while the stock fell 88% from
> $79 to $9.50. Heavy infrastructure capex is **contractually and physically rigid** —
> land, permits, cooling, cross-border supply chains, multi-year builds, sunk cost and
> breakup fees. So capex leads on the way up and **lags badly** on the way down.
> **股價必然在上游賣鏟人當期營收最輝煌、利潤最肥、產能最緊的那一刻見頂.**
> 盯絕對值 = 盯後視鏡開車。

### 第四步 · 看燃料
Weekly, read one line of the Fed's **H.4.1**: **準備金餘額**.
Above **$2.8T**, the melt-up's liquidity is sufficient. A fast break below **$2.5T**
with no crisis to explain it → **無條件清倉，沒有討論餘地**.

### 第五步 · 看時間
Take the **8–12 週** shakeout-to-reconcentration window (consistent across his three
historical samples), lay it over the macro calendar, and wait for **偏度倒掛 + 放量還
抗跌**. That is the steepest part of the second wave. Keep the capex second-derivative
as the **唯一逃生鈴**.

## 三開關

| # | 開關 | 讀什麼 | 綠燈 | 動作線 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **燃料** | Fed H.4.1 準備金餘額 (FRED `WRESBAL`) | > $2.8T | 無危機下快速跌破 **$2.5T** → 無條件清倉 |
| 2 | **點火** | 選擇權 put/call 偏度極端倒掛 + 現貨開始抗跌 | 偏度倒掛出現 | 代表做市商被迫掃貨、弱手已洗乾淨 → 二波點火倒數 |
| 3 | **逃生** | 下游雲廠商 capex **環比**增速 | 還在加速/持平 | **見頂回落 → 立刻平倉**，哪怕當期淨利再創新高 |

Switch 3 is the one that matters most and the one people skip. His reasoning: the
$18B of prepayments and take-or-pay contracts are priced as **增量估值** — a valuation
of the *increment*. **增量沒了，估值就沒了。**

## 他自己的底部確認清單（湊齊三個以上才算底）

From the 2026-06-08 analysis, quoted back in the replay. Useful independently of
the second-wave setup:

1. 做市商 **Gamma 分佈翻正** — the master switch; only then does dealer hedging flip
   from amplifying the fall to cushioning it.
2. **VIX 期限結構從倒掛回到 Contango** — far-dated vol back above near-dated = panic ebbing.
3. **加密資金費率轉正**、未平倉合約止穩 — the speculative bid is alive again.
4. **CTA 的機械拋壓釋放完畢**（他估一輪去槓桿約 $800 億），指數重新站上關鍵位。
5. **全局相關性見頂回落** — 個股回到各走各的基本面，而不是齊漲齊跌.

> 在那之前不管跌得多深，都還在流動性真空的劇本裡，千萬別接。

## 為什麼華爾街停不下來（第三步的背景）

Three mechanical bids that ignore valuation, which is why "明知是泡沫還是往上推":

1. **相對收益考核** — funds are graded against the benchmark, not on absolute return.
   With a handful of mega-caps carrying huge index weight, *not* holding them is the
   bigger career risk. 職業風險 > 客戶本金風險.
2. **被動 ETF 飛輪** — passive money buys by market-cap weight and never looks at
   valuation. Price up → weight up → more forced buying.
3. **Gamma 擠壓** — dealers short calls must buy spot to stay delta-neutral; as price
   rises, gamma rises, and the hedging window is *mathematically forced* wider. This is
   the most violent leg and the actual detonator of a second wave.

> 前花旗 CEO Prince: **"只要音樂還在放，你就必須站起來跳舞。"**
